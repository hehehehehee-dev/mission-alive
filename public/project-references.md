# Mission Alive — project references

## SI / AI disclosure

Super Intelligence (SI) / AI assistance: OpenAI Codex assisted implementation, debugging, test design, translations, narrative drafts, and exploration of presentation and gameplay options. Original programmatic illustrations and simulations were built with AI coding assistance. ElevenLabs generated the four machine narration recordings. Those voices are synthetic, not NASA recordings. NASA and partner-agency images, observations, data and archive audio are credited separately. No standalone generative-image or generative-video assets were added.

Project requirements, supplied voice assets, feedback and publication decisions come from the project author. The disclosure above describes the AI assistance used during development.
