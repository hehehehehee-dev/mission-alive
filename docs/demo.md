## One-sentence pitch

Four machines cannot come home, so Mission Alive lets learners train on their real jobs, collect their transmissions, and carry one story home.

## 60-second edited overview

- 0–8 seconds: show `CREW BRIEFING`, the four training modules and `CREW CERTIFICATION` at 0/4. Introduce why this museum's machines cannot be brought indoors.
- 8–20: show an excerpt of Curiosity's command plan, analyzed outcrop and evidence-supported habitability report; reveal its transmission.
- 20–30: show Ingenuity's onboard controller, 70% rotor power, fixed trim target and safe flat-plain landing.
- 30–40: open Webb's wings, align the three images and reveal the infrared view.
- 40–52: play an original Voyager greeting using keyboard input, type and save a greeting, then show its stamp announcement. Rotation and outward travel are optional. Label the JPL display as a range estimate and the dated NASA milestone as the fallback.
- 52–60: show `TRAINING COMPLETE`, all four stamps, and the existing printable certificate. End with the invitation to tell one machine's story to one other person.

Use recorded excerpts and label edits honestly; this overview is not a claim that a first-time learner can finish all four modules in one minute.

## Project-page draft

Mission Alive is an independent educational museum for students aged 10–14. A crew briefing sends learners into four training modules: program and debug Curiosity's science route, plan Ingenuity's flight and landing, unfold and align Webb before revealing infrared light, and listen and contribute to a Golden Record experience.

Each module connects an action to a visible consequence, a NASA source and a reflection question. The existing four stamps appear as crew certification; earning each reveals a first-person transmission. Four stamps open graduation and the existing name-and-date certificate. The hybrid framing preserves the working mechanics and local progress model.

Vite, vanilla JavaScript, Three.js and Web Audio power the core static museum without required accounts. Optional server functions supply live JPL estimates and moderated classroom/community records. This version's geometry and controls are simplified. It has not yet been evaluated with students, and no improvement in learning outcomes is claimed. Keyboard or screen-reader verification must be supported by actual test evidence in `docs/verification.md`. AI assistance is documented in the repository.
